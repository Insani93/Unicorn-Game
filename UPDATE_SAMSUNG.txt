UNICORN MAGIC WORLD v5 — SIX WORLD MUSIC

1. Download and extract ZIP using Samsung My Files.
2. Open your GitHub game repository in Chrome.
3. Add file -> Upload files -> select ALL extracted files (not the ZIP).
4. Replace files where needed, commit changes.
5. Reload the game. Do NOT clear browser site data/progress.
6. Tap Play Adventure > Explore All Worlds > 🎵 Hear theme.
7. Number or letter Free Play now automatically changes music to match its world.
8. Worlds 2–6 story missions are not playable yet. Themes are previewable.
